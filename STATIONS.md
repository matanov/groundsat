# Ground stations and their sources

Every agency and commercial ground station on [groundsat.com](https://groundsat.com), with the source behind its position and antennas. Generated from the data the site loads; agency stations compiled 2026-10-22, commercial 2026-10-24. SatNOGS stations (about 600) come live from the [SatNOGS Network](https://network.satnogs.org) and aren't listed here.

How to read it:
- **Position level:** "site" is the station itself; "city" or "region" means the operator names only a town or region, so the marker is that place, not the antennas.
- **(assumed):** the operator doesn't publish a dish size or bands, so a typical value is used (3.7 m dish, S/X bands) and labelled as such in the app.
- **Wikipedia** links are pinned to the revision that was read.

Something wrong or missing? [Report it](../../issues/new?template=2-station.yml) with a source.
